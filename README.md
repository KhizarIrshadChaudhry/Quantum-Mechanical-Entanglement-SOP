# SOP Thesis: Quantum Mechanical Entanglement

## About the project

This repository contains my **SOP (Studieområdeprojekt)**, the major
interdisciplinary project completed during **3.G of the Danish HTX
(Higher Technical Examination Programme)** at NEXT Sukkertoppen HTX.

The project combines **Physics A** and **Mathematics A** and focuses on
**quantum mechanical entanglement**, with particular emphasis on
**Bell's inequality (CHSH)**.

The project covers: - The fundamental concepts of quantum mechanical
entanglement, local realism, determinism, and local hidden variables. -
The mathematical foundations needed to describe the problem, including
complex numbers, vector spaces, inner products, and tensor products. -
Classical and quantum-mechanical derivations of Bell's inequality and
the CHSH S-parameter. - Analysis of results from a **Bell's inequality
experiment conducted at DTU Nanoteket**. - A discussion of what the
experimental results imply for local realism and determinism. - A
perspective on modern applications of quantum entanglement, including
**quantum cryptography**.

The project was completed as part of my final-year academic work in HTX
and combines theoretical mathematics and physics with experimental data
analysis.
